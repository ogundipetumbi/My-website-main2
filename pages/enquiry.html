Understand Your Hair Porosity

Hair porosity describes how well your hair can absorb and retain moisture. Understanding your hair porosity can help you choose products that are suitable for your hair.

Low Porosity Hair

Hair with low porosity has tightly closed cuticles and may take longer to absorb moisture.

Lightweight products and regular hydration can help prevent product build-up.

Medium Porosity Hair

Medium porosity hair generally absorbs and retains moisture well.

A balanced hair care routine can help maintain healthy-looking hair.

High Porosity Hair

High porosity hair can absorb moisture quickly but may also lose moisture easily.

Moisturising products and sealing techniques can help maintain hydration.
